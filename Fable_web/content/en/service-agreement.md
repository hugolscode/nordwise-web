Early termination of an active engagement may result in:

- Immediate suspension of all pending processes, filings, and applications.
- Forfeiture of all amounts paid to date.
- Legal action to recover outstanding fees if a payment plan is in place.

### Arrears and Non-Payment
If the Client is **30 or more days in arrears**, Nordwise reserves the right to suspend all services, resign as registered agent (where applicable), and notify the relevant governmental authorities of such action.

Engagements on payment plans where no payment has been received for **60 consecutive days** will be considered cancelled due to non-payment and subject to the early cancellation terms above.
