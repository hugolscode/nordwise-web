faq:
  - q: Do I need to be a US citizen to open an LLC?
    a: No. Anyone, regardless of nationality or country of residence, can form an LLC in the United States.
  - q: How long does it take to open a US LLC?
    a: Formation takes 1-3 weeks depending on the state. The EIN may take an additional 2-4 weeks. The full process is usually completed within 4-6 weeks.
  - q: Do I need to travel to the US?
    a: No. The entire process is 100% remote. No travel, no in-person signatures required.
  - q: Does a non-resident pay taxes in the US?
    a: A single-member LLC owned by a non-resident, with no employees or physical presence in the US, generally does not pay US income tax. Every case is reviewed in the initial consultation.
  - q: Which US state is best for non-residents?
    a: New Mexico is usually the most cost-effective. Wyoming offers strong privacy and Delaware is preferred by startups. The right choice depends on your case.
  - q: What bank account can I open with my LLC?
    a: With your LLC and EIN you can open accounts with Mercury, Relay or Wise Business, or activate Stripe. We advise you on what fits your business best.
