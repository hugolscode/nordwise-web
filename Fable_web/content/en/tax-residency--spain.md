faq_tag: FAQ
faq_title: Common questions | *about Spain.*
faq:
  - q: What is the Beckham law and is it right for me?
    a: It's a special regime allowing new residents to be taxed as non-residents (flat 24% on employment income up to €600,000) during the arrival year and the five following years. It's a great fit in many cases, but not all, it depends on where your income comes from.
  - q: I have a US LLC and I'm moving to Spain, what happens?
    a: It depends on how it's structured and who manages it. An LLC managed from Spain can be deemed a Spanish tax resident entity. It's exactly the kind of situation to review before the move.
  - q: What is the Spanish tax quarantine?
    a: If you're a Spanish national moving to a jurisdiction deemed a tax haven, the tax authority can keep treating you as tax resident for the year of the move plus the four following years. That's why destination choice and documentation are key.
