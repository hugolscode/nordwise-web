faq_tag: FAQ
faq_title: What we get | *asked the most.*
faq:
  - q: Is it legal to change tax residency to pay less tax?
    a: Yes, as long as the move is real and well documented. What doesn't work is faking a relocation. Our job is to make your change solid and defensible.
  - q: How much time do I need to spend in the new country?
    a: It depends on both countries involved. The famous 183-day rule is only part of the picture, economic and family ties also count. We go through it in detail in your consultation.
  - q: What about my home country when I leave?
    a: The exit needs to be handled properly, tax deregistration, residency certificates and, in some countries, special rules like Spain's tax quarantine. We leave it all closed for you.
  - q: Can I combine a residency change with a US LLC?
    a: Yes, it's actually a common combination. The LLC structures your income and your residency defines where you pay tax. We design both pieces so they fit together.
