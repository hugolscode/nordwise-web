Forming the LLC is only half the job. The other half, the one that turns a piece of paper into an operating company, is called the EIN.

## What the EIN is

The EIN (Employer Identification Number) is the tax ID the IRS assigns to your company. Nine digits that identify your LLC to banks, payment processors and the IRS itself.

## What you need it for

Without an EIN, your LLC can do almost nothing:

- **Open a bank account.** Mercury, Relay and the rest always require it.
- **Activate Stripe or PayPal Business.** Essential to get paid.
- **File returns.** Your LLC's annual informational filings are made under the EIN.
- **Sign W-8/W-9 forms.** The forms American clients and platforms will ask you for.

## Can I get one without an SSN?

Yes. This is the point that causes the most confusion. **You do not need a Social Security Number or ITIN** to get your LLC's EIN. The non-resident process is done by filing Form SS-4 with the IRS, by fax or mail, and it requires knowing exactly how to fill it in so it doesn't get rejected.

The usual timeline for non-residents is **2 to 4 weeks**, depending on IRS workload.
